Our Story

Tampa-Made Since 2008

HitMaster Graphics was founded with a single purpose: to give promotional products distributors and print brokers a reliable, high-quality production partner that they could trust to represent their brand — invisibly and impeccably.

Starting with a single press on West Fig Street in Tampa, we've grown into a full-service decoration facility offering screen printing, embroidery, direct-to-garment, and end-to-end fulfillment — all in-house, all under one roof.

Our model is simple: you sell, we produce. We never contact your clients. We ship under your brand. Your success is our success.

B2B First

We Work for You, Not Around You

We built HitMaster specifically to serve the trade — promotional products distributors, print brokers, ad agencies, and resellers. That means every business decision we make is designed to protect your interests:

  • ✓
    Blind shipping on every order
    No HitMaster branding on packages, packing slips, or return labels.
  • ✓
    No consumer price lists
    Our pricing is never advertised to the public. Only registered brokers have access.
  • ✓
    NDAs available on request
    For accounts requiring additional confidentiality protections.
  • ✓
    We never approach your clients
    Your relationships are yours. We have no interest in your client list.
