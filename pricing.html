Common Questions

Pricing FAQs

Are blanks included in these prices?+
No. All pricing shown is for decoration only. Blank apparel is priced separately based on brand, style, color, and size. We work with all major suppliers (S&S, SanMar, Alpha Broder, etc.) or can decorate blanks you supply.
Is there a minimum order quantity?+
Screen printing has a 12-piece minimum per design. Embroidery has a 1-piece minimum (though pricing improves significantly at 12+). DTG has no minimum — order as few as 1 piece.
Are setup/screen charges one-time or recurring?+
Screen setup charges ($25/color) apply to each new order. Screens are not stored indefinitely. Embroidery digitizing ($25) is a one-time fee — your DST file is kept on file for future orders.
Do you offer volume discounts beyond the price breaks shown?+
Yes. For orders exceeding 1,000 pieces or for high-volume broker partners, custom pricing is available. Contact our sales team for a dedicated pricing agreement.
