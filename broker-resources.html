Art & File Preparation Guidelines

ℹ️ Submitting print-ready files saves time and avoids art fees. If your art needs prep work, our in-house team can assist — just let us know in your order notes.

✅ Preferred File Formats

  • ✓Adobe Illustrator (.AI, .EPS) — Vector, outlines converted, all text outlined
  • ✓PDF (vector) — Fonts embedded/outlined, no transparency issues
  • ✓High-res PNG — 300 DPI minimum at print size, transparent background
  • ✓PSD (Photoshop) — Layers preserved, 300 DPI at actual print size

❌ File Types to Avoid

  • ✗Low-resolution JPGs from websites (72 DPI)
  • ✗Word documents (.DOC) or PowerPoint files
  • ✗Screenshots or screen captures
  • ✗Fonts not outlined / converted to curves
