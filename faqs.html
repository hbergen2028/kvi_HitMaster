Getting Started

Do you sell to the general public?+
No. HitMaster Graphics is a strictly B2B wholesale operation. We work exclusively with promotional products distributors, print brokers, ad agencies, graphic designers, and resellers. We do not accept retail orders from end consumers.
How do I become a broker partner?+
Simply fill out our Broker Registration form. We'll review your application within 1–2 business days and send you account credentials and pricing access. You'll need a valid business license or resale certificate.
Can I place an order before my broker account is approved?+
Yes, on a limited basis. You can submit a quote request as a new customer without a registered account. However, registered broker partners receive wholesale pricing tiers, access to the free freight program, and priority order processing.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, AmEx), ACH bank transfer, and check. Established broker accounts may qualify for Net-30 credit terms — apply via the Credit Application in Broker Resources.

Pricing & Orders

Does your pricing include the blank apparel?+
No. All decoration pricing shown on our Pricing page is for the print/decoration service only. Blank garments are billed separately, either sourced through us (we work with S&S Activewear, SanMar, Alpha Broder, and others) or you can ship us your own blanks.
What are your minimum order quantities?+
Screen printing: 12-piece minimum per design/color combination. Embroidery: 1-piece minimum (though pricing improves significantly at 12+). DTG: no minimum — order as few as 1 piece. For screen printing, mixing sizes or garment colors counts toward the minimum as long as the design is the same.
Are screen setup charges waived on reorders?+
Screen setup fees ($25/color) apply to each new production run. Screens from previous jobs are not permanently retained. For high-frequency repeat orders, ask your account rep about screen storage options.
Can I mix garment colors without additional charges?+
For screen printing, mixing garment colors in the same order is generally not a problem as long as the ink color works on all garments. If a dark garment requires an underbase and a light garment doesn't, additional setup charges may apply. Ask us when submitting your order.
Do you offer custom pricing for high-volume accounts?+
Yes. Accounts placing 1,000+ pieces per month or significant annual volume can negotiate a custom pricing agreement. Contact our sales team at sales@hitmastergraphics.com.

Artwork & Files

What file formats do you accept?+
Preferred: Adobe Illustrator (.AI, .EPS), vector PDFs, high-resolution PNGs (300+ DPI at print size), and PSD files. We can work from JPEG files if they are high resolution, but vector formats always produce the best results. See our Art Guidelines for full specs.
Do you offer art services?+
Yes. Our in-house art team provides color separations, vectorization, embroidery digitizing, and basic design assistance. Art fees vary by complexity — typically $25–$75 for most jobs. We'll quote any art work before proceeding.
Do you keep art files on file for reorders?+
Yes. Embroidery DST files and approved screen separations are kept on file for registered accounts. Reference your previous order number when reordering the same design. Files are retained for at least 12 months from last use.
Will I receive a proof before production?+
Yes. A digital art proof is sent to you for approval before any job goes to production. Production does not begin until you provide written (email) approval. For embroidery on new designs, a physical sewout sample is available on request.

Production

When does my turnaround time start?+
Turnaround begins after: (1) your art proof is approved, AND (2) payment or purchase order is received. Delays in art approval or payment will delay production. We strongly recommend submitting print-ready art to avoid any hold-ups.
What is your policy on misprints or defects?+
We inspect every order before shipping. Industry standard allows a spoilage tolerance of 1–2% on screen print jobs. If you receive defective items beyond this tolerance, contact us within 5 business days with photos. We will reprint or credit the defective pieces.
Can I supply my own blank garments?+
Yes, we decorate customer-supplied goods (CSG). However, please note that CSG orders carry additional risk — if a garment is damaged during production, HitMaster's liability is limited to the decoration cost, not the garment cost. Ship garments to our Tampa facility with your order number clearly marked.
What inks and threads do you use?+
Screen print: plastisol inks (Wilflex, Union, Ryonet brands), water-based, and discharge inks. We offer specialty inks including neon, metallic, puff, glitter, and glow-in-the-dark. Embroidery: Madeira Polyneon and Isacord thread. Color charts available in Broker Resources.

Shipping & Fulfillment

Do you ship blind (without HitMaster branding)?+
Absolutely. All shipments are blind-shipped by default. No HitMaster Graphics branding appears on the box, packing slip, or return label. We can ship with your company name as the return address on request.
What carriers do you use?+
We primarily use UPS and FedEx for standard and expedited shipments. USPS is available for small packages. Freight carriers are used for large palletized orders. We can ship on your carrier account number if you provide it.
How does the Free Freight Program work?+
Registered broker partners who enroll in the Free Freight Program receive free ground shipping on qualifying orders that meet the threshold. Enrollment requires signing the freight program agreement (available in Broker Resources). Contact your account rep for current threshold details.
Do you offer drop shipping to multiple addresses?+
Yes. Our fulfillment service supports multi-destination drop ship. Submit a ship-to list (template available in Broker Resources) with your order and we'll handle individual shipping to each recipient. Per-package handling fees apply for multi-destination orders.

Broker Program

Is there a cost to join the broker program?+
No. Broker registration is free. There are no membership fees, monthly minimums, or setup costs. You simply need to be a legitimate business in the print, promotional products, or related industry.
Will HitMaster ever contact my clients directly?+
Never. We operate strictly as your invisible production partner. We have no interest in your client relationships and will never reach out to, market to, or solicit your clients. NDAs are available on request for accounts requiring additional assurance.
What is your policy on competitor pricing?+
Our wholesale pricing is not published publicly — it's only available to registered broker partners. If you find a comparable wholesale price elsewhere, contact your account rep. We're committed to remaining competitive and will work to match or beat legitimate competing quotes.
How quickly are new broker applications approved?+
Most applications are reviewed and approved within 1–2 business days. You'll receive an email with your account number, pricing access, and instructions for submitting your first order.
