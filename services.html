Service 03

Direct-to-Garment (DTG)

No screens, no minimums, no color limits. DTG is perfect for photorealistic prints, complex gradients, and short-run specialty items where screen printing isn't cost-effective.

ℹ️ Best for: Art with 8+ colors, photorealistic images, low quantities (1–47 pieces), and same-day proofing.
  • ✓ Unlimited colors — no color fees
  • ✓ No screen setup charges
  • ✓ 100% cotton and cotton-poly blends
  • ✓ White ink available for dark garments
  • ✓ 1 piece minimum
